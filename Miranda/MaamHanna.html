My Mentor

The moment you entered the classroom, the atmosphere had became skittish,
The one-liners, the wit, the mindless scrolling had seemed to vanish
"It was as if a tyrant had conquered the land," I anxiously assumed,
A few weeks had passed, and much was shown more than what I considered...

Reassuring ourselves that mistakes can be learned and are inevitable,
Boosting ourselves to the path of regulation, accountability, and the imperceptible,
Through the glass past the fog, I can envision
A courteous, disciplined lecturer who compromises for her students by pure determination

Thank you for the hard work and care you have shone for all of us students.

To whom wrote this poem, thou wonder?
A hint, exactly three, were given:
an Archangel, associated with Thursday, wealth, and prosperity
The embodiment of the West Wind, apart of the Greek Anemoi,
A victory that is covered by laurel,
And finally, a Spanish surname, it deems itself worthy of admiration amidst the Tempest.
